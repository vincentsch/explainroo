# Send an invoice in Little Ledger

## intro
This is how you send an invoice in Little Ledger.

## demo
[#list] All your invoices sit in one list. To bill a customer, [#new] click New invoice. [#customer] Pick who pays, [#amount] type the amount it comes to, [#note] and add a short note. [#check] Check the form once more before you go on, [#save] then save it, and the form closes. [#lands] The invoice lands at the end of the list, [#total] and the total goes up with it.

## outro {hold=1.5}
One list, one button, and the invoice is on its way.
