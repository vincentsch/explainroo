# Why ice floats

## question {lead=0.2}
Almost every solid [#sink] sinks in its own liquid. [#ice] Ice does not. [#why] So why does it float?

## molecules
In liquid water, the molecules [#move] move around and pack close together. [#freeze] When water freezes, [#lattice] each molecule holds on to its neighbors in a fixed pattern, with [#gaps] open space in it.

## number
That open space makes ice lighter for its size. [#number] Ice is about nine percent less dense than water, [#float] so it floats.

## lakes
That matters for life. A lake [#top] freezes from the top down. [#lid] The ice covers the water like a lid, and [#fish] the fish below make it through the winter.

## summary {hold=1.2}
[#sum] So, to sum up. [#s1] Frozen water takes more space. [#s2] That makes it lighter than water. [#s3] And that keeps lakes alive in winter.
