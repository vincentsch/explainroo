# Acme Notes

## hook {lead=0.2}
[#name] Meet Acme Notes. [#promise] It turns the notes of a meeting into a clear list of tasks.

## problem
After a meeting, the notes are [#scattered] spread over three apps. [#lost] The tasks in them get lost, and [#nobody] nobody knows who does what.

## demo
[#paste] Paste your notes into Acme Notes, and [#click] click Make tasks. [#list] A moment later, every task has a name and a date.

## features
It does three things. It [#f1] finds every task, it [#f2] says who does it, and it [#f3] reminds them before the date.
