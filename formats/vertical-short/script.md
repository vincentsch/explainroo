# Why your phone dies in the cold

## hook {lead=0}
[#hook] Your phone says [#twenty] twenty percent, [#cold] then it [#dies] dies in the cold.

## why
But the charge is [#still] still there. [#slow] Cold slows down the battery's [#chem] chemistry, [#power] so it can't give the phone enough power.

## turn
[#turn] Here's the good part. [#warm] Warm the phone up, and [#back] the percent comes back.

## tip {hold=1}
[#pocket] Keep it in an inside pocket. [#charge] And never charge it while it's frozen, [#harm] because that can harm the battery.
